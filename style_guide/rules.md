# Tafsir-Roman-Urdu (TRU) Linguistic Style Guide & Orthography Specification

## 1. Project Philosophy & Guiding Principles

The **Tafsir-Roman-Urdu (TRU)** project provides the world's first complete, standardized Roman Urdu transliteration of **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (translated by Maulana Muhammad Junagarhi). 

### Inviolable Core Principles
1. **Zero Hallucination / Zero Omission**: The transliteration must be an exact, faithful transliteration of the authentic Urdu text—never summarized, abridged, paraphrased, or reinterpreted.
2. **Readability & Standard Phonology**: Text must be instantly natural and legible to speakers of Urdu/Hindi who read Latin script, without archaic or esoteric diacritics while preserving phonetic integrity.
3. **Reverence for Sacred Texts**: All divine names, Quranic citations, Hadith terms, and Islamic honorifics must be rendered with standardized orthography.

## 2. Vowel System & Orthography

### 2.1 Short Vowels (Harakat)
- **Zabar (َ - Fatha)**: `a` (e.g., *Kalam*, *Sanad*, *Haqq*)
- **Zer (ِ - Kasra)**: `i` (e.g., *Isi*, *Kitab*, *Milna*)
- **Pesh (ُ - Damma)**: `u` (e.g., *Hukm*, *Dua*, *Kuh*)

### 2.2 Long Vowels
- **Alif / Alif Madda (آ / ا)**: `aa` or `a` (e.g., *Aayat*, *Aasman*, *Qur'an*, *Sahaba*)
- **Chhoti Ye (ای - Ma'roof)**: `ee` or `i` consistently (e.g., *Tafseer*, *Hadees*, *Nabi*, *Deen*)
- **Waw Ma'roof (او)**: `oo` or `u` (e.g., *Rasool*, *Noor*, *Suboot*, *Qool*)

### 2.3 Majhool Vowels (Special to Urdu)
- **Bari Ye (ے - Majhool)**: `e` (e.g., *Yeh*, *Khel*, *Dene*, *Karein*, *Lage*)
- **Waw Majhool (او - Majhool)**: `o` (e.g., *Woh*, *Log*, *Roz*, *Hona*, *Dono*)

### 2.4 Diphthongs
- **Ai / Ay (ای)**: `ai` / `ay` (e.g., *Jaise*, *Aise*, *Paida*, *Ghair*)
- **Au (او)**: `au` (e.g., *Aur*, *Khauf*, *Maut*)

### 2.5 Noon Ghunna (ں - Nasal N)
Rendered as `n` without trailing hard consonant:
- *Hain* (ہیں)
- *Nahin* (نہیں)
- *Karein* (کریں)
- *Jahan* (جہاں)
- *Dilon* (دلوں)
- *Unhein* (انہیں)

### 2.6 Tashdeed (ّ - Gemination)
Double the corresponding consonant to reflect doubled sound:
- *Allah*, *Muhammad*, *Jannat*, *Jahannam*, *Rabbi*, *Ittefaq*, *Kuffar*, *Siddeeq*

### 2.7 Hamzah (ء) and Ayn (ع)
- Represented with a crisp apostrophe `'` where phonetically distinct:
  - *Qur'an*, *Ka'bah*, *Isti'azah*, *Du'a*, *'Aalameen*, *Ta'ala*, *Ma'na*, *Razi'ah*

## 3. Compound Forms & Izafat (اضافت)

### 3.1 Izafat-e-Kasra
Use hyphenated lowercase `-e-`:
- *Amr-e-Ilahi* (امر الٰہی)
- *Ahl-e-Kitab* (اہل کتاب)
- *Rahmat-e-Khuda* (رحمت خدا)
- *Roz-e-Qayamat* (روز قیامت)
- *Dastoor-e-Zindagi* (دستور زندگی)

### 3.2 Izafat-e-Waw
Use hyphenated lowercase `-o-`:
- *Lail-o-Nahar* (لیل و نہار)
- *Subh-o-Sham* (صبح و شام)
- *Khurd-o-Nosh* (خورد و نوش)

### 3.3 Common Islamic Compound Terms
- *Kitabullah* (کتاب اللہ)
- *Bani Israel* (بنی اسرائیل)
- *Baitullah* (بیت اللہ)
- *Insha'Allah* (ان شاء اللہ)
- *Masha'Allah* (ما شاء اللہ)
- *SubhanAllah* (سبحان اللہ)
- *Alhamdulillah* (الحمد للہ)
- *Astaghfirullah* (استغفر اللہ)
- *JazakAllahu Khairan* (جزاک اللہ خیرا)

## 4. Sacred Islamic Honorifics (Salawaat & Razi)

All honorific abbreviations or Arabic ligatures in the source Urdu must be expanded uniformly:

| Source Script | Normalized Roman Urdu Equivalent | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ﷺ` / `صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم` | `(ﷺ / Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam)` | Peace and blessings of Allah be upon him |
| `رضی اللہ عنہ` / `ؓ` (male) | `(Razi Allahu Anhu / R.A)` | May Allah be pleased with him |
| `رضی اللہ عنہا` / `ؓ` (female) | `(Razi Allahu Anha / R.A)` | May Allah be pleased with her |
| `رضی اللہ عنہم` / `ؓ` (plural) | `(Razi Allahu Anhum / R.A)` | May Allah be pleased with them |
| `علیہ السلام` / `ؑ` | `(Alaihis Salam / A.S)` | Peace be upon him |
| `علیہم السلام` / `ؑ` (plural) | `(Alaihimas Salam / A.S)` | Peace be upon them |
| `رحمہ اللہ` / `ؒ` | `(Rahimahullah / R.H)` | May Allah have mercy upon him |
| `رحمہم اللہ` / `ؒ` (plural) | `(Rahimahumullah / R.H)` | May Allah have mercy upon them |
| `سبحانہ و تعالی` / `تعالیٰ` | `Ta'ala` or `(Subhanahu Wa Ta'ala)` | Glorified and Exalted is He |

## 6. Formatting & Structural Invariants

1. **Paragraph Continuity**: Double newline (`\n\n`) separates sections, thematic headings, or long commentary paragraphs.
2. **Verse Citations**: Retain chapter and verse numerical citations accurately, e.g., `(16:98)` or `(Surah Al-Baqarah: 255)`.
3. **Embedded Arabic**: Arabic quotations (Quranic verses and Hadith texts) should be transliterated faithfully into classical Roman Arabic in parentheses or quotes alongside the Urdu commentary.
4. **Encoding**: Strictly UTF-8 without BOM.
